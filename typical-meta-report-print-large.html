Method and definitions
  • Source: Meta Ads via Supermetrics, pulled 9 Sep 2026. The 7 day window includes ads over $25 spend, the 30 day window ads over $50.
  • ROAS is purchase conversion value divided by spend. Meta's own ROAS field is not used, because it averages daily ratios and returned figures as high as 1,387 on single ads in this account.
  • Target ROAS is 2.00. The most we can pay per order is AOV divided by that target, so it moves between windows as AOV moves.
  • Hook = 3-second views / impressions. Completion = 100% plays / 25% plays. The 25% base is used rather than 3-second views, because the two counts come from different bases in this account.
  • Reach and frequency are not shown. Meta cannot return deduplicated reach at ad level in this grouping.
  • Calls: Scale is at or above 2.00 with three or more purchases. Test up is at or above 2.00 with fewer. Fix is 1.00 to 2.00. Pause is under 1.00 with $200 or more spent. Watch is under $200 spent. Awareness and engagement ads sit outside the ROAS calls.
  • Previews use the live creative asset with its own primary text and headline. Video ads show the cover frame. Each preview is letterboxed inside a fixed frame so nothing is stretched or cropped.
